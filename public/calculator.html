2 Sales Comparables

Manual search only — this never runs automatically. Before clicking, confirm the Sq. Ft., Beds, Baths, and Year Built fields in Subject Property above actually describe this property (not just whatever was left in those fields); the search matches comps against those exact values, so stale or default numbers will pull the wrong comps without any warning other than what you set. Once you click, it finds the 3 comps that most closely match the subject's property type, square footage, beds and baths, within 1.5–2 miles — prioritizing closed sales in the last 90 days, then widening to 6 and up to 12 months across a 1.5–3 mile radius until 3 confirmed sales are found. Every candidate must clear two gates: (1) it can't be a currently active listing, and (2) it must have an actual county-recorded deed sale (SOLD badge, with the closing date) inside that time window — properties that are pending, under contract, accepting backup offers, withdrawn, or expired without selling all fail gate 2 and are dropped. In thin markets the search will reach back up to 12 months to hit 3 comps; the status message shows exactly how far it reached. Among qualifying comps, ones biased toward recently remodeled/updated resales — using price/sq.ft. above the local comp pool's median as the proxy ( rows below) — are favored slightly, since that better reflects a fix-and-flip's post-renovation condition. Always confirm condition and lot size against MLS photos / your own walkthrough before relying on these.
